    Methods and rounding

    Rate (mL/h) = dose ÷ concentration, with ×weight for per-kg units and ×60 for per-minute units. Concentration is in the dose's mass unit per mL (mcg/mL, mg/mL or units/mL). Reverse: dose = rate × concentration (÷60 for per-minute, ÷weight for per-kg).

    Display rounding. Rates below 5 mL/h show to 0.1 mL/h, otherwise to the whole mL/h; the 5 mL/h cut is decided on the unrounded rate. Literature model: ties round half-up (4.25 → 4.3, 22.5 → 23). Site model: ties round half-to-even exactly as the site's VBScript Round does on the raw double (4.25 → 4.2, 22.5 → 22, 37.5 → 38), and the titration rows are generated by the same stepped loop (dose += step), which reproduces all 732 live-server rows. The printable chart in the site model reproduces the PDF charts instead (half-up, with propofol, bivalirudin and eptifibatide always to 0.1 mL/h).

    Loading volumes. Literature: to 0.1 mL (half-up). Site: same rule as rates (server).

    Weight. Literature: lb → kg with PK.body.lbToKg (× 0.45359237 exactly; rounded for display only). Site: ÷ 2.2 and rounded to 0.1 kg, which is what the site echoes back (100 lb → 45.5 kg).

    Limits. A dose above the maximum is a hard stop: no rate is offered. A dose outside the usual range is a soft warning. Renal and hepatic rules give a recommended dose; ordering above it is a warning, and eptifibatide in a dialysis-dependent patient is a hard stop. Literature hard maxima come from the product labels where a label states one (dopamine 50, dobutamine 40, esmolol 300, epinephrine 2 mcg/kg/min; milrinone 0.75 mcg/kg/min; nicardipine and diltiazem 15 mg/h; vasopressin 0.1 units/min; amiodarone 1 mg/min continuous). Where no label maximum exists the limit is Institutional — P&T and can be changed on this page for the session (heparin 35 units/kg/h and 2,500 units/h, insulin 30 units/h, nitroglycerin 400 mcg/min, norepinephrine 3 mcg/kg/min, propofol 80 mcg/kg/min). Sources: decisions/acyclovir-infusions.md Q6–Q10.

    Argatroban (literature). Novastan EU SmPC two-track table: standard track starts at 2 mcg/kg/min (aPTT < 1.5 × baseline → +0.5; next aPTT in 2 h); critically ill or hepatic track (Child-Pugh ≥ 7) starts at 0.5 (+0.1; next aPTT in 4 h). aPTT > 3 × baseline or > 100 s: stop until back in range, then resume at half the previous dose. Target 1.5–3 × baseline, ≤ 100 s; maximum 10 mcg/kg/min (US label). The site model keeps the site's fixed 40–83 s table.

    Procainamide load (literature). 17 mg/kg on actual weight capped at 100 kg (institutional) is the hard stop; above 1 g is a soft alert (label maximum advisable dose); loading rate default 20 mg/min, hard maximum 50 mg/min.

    Eptifibatide weight cap. Literature: above 121 kg the label's fixed maximum is used (bolus 22.6 mg = 11.3 mL; 15 mg/h = 20 mL/h at 2 mcg/kg/min, 7.5 mg/h = 10 mL/h at 1). Site: weight is capped at 121 kg in the formula (bolus 11 mL, 19 and 10 mL/h).
