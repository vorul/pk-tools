Vancomycin Charts & Hemodialysis

Trough charts (Chart I and II) and the high-flux HD protocol follow Marshall Pierce, PharmD, at pkineticdrugdosing.com, with literature corrections (Rybak 2020). Part of the PK Suite. For AUC and Bayesian dosing use vancomycin.html.

Trough-only dosing is no longer recommended (Rybak 2020): AUC24 400–600 mg·h/L is the standard. The trough charts are a legacy and educational tool. The HD section uses pre-HD levels, which the guideline still supports.

Retrospective adjustment: one steady-state trough ("fit K")

Valid only at steady state (after 3–5 doses). Vd is held at 0.65 L/kg (the chart value, at any BMI) and only K is fitted, which is the method Vanchart validates (true Vd 0.4–0.9 L/kg). This non-Bayesian trough-only fit is not a guideline method verify.

Chart model and sources

Kel = 0.00107 × CrCl_norm + 0.0052106005 (chart header), CrCl_norm = CrCl × 1.73 / BSA (DuBois), Vd = 0.65 L/kg TBW, infusion time T′ = 1.5 h (back-calculated; not printed on the chart).

Cmin,ss = Dose(mg/kg)·(1 − e^(−K·T′))·e^(−K(τ − T′)) / (0.65·K·T′·(1 − e^(−K·τ))). Regenerated cells match 569 of 600 published cells (τ 8/12/24) exactly; the other 31 (Chart I, mostly CrCl ≤ 15) differ by 0.1–0.3 mcg/mL.

CrCl: Cockcroft-Gault with LBW (Devine) = min(LBW, TBW) in the Site model; the suite literature policy (AdjBW if TBW > 1.2 × IBW) in the Literature model. Heights under 60 in clamp the LBW height term at 0. Dosing weight and Vd use TBW.

Patient and dialysis

Settings

Level timing

  • Draw a level after the loading dose, before the first HD, to confirm an adequate load.
  • Pre-HD sampling is preferred. If a level is drawn after dialysis, wait at least 6 h post-HD: earlier levels are falsely low (redistribution up to 12 h, 20–40% rebound).
  • Without a loading dose, levels take about 2 weeks to stabilize.
  • Give post-HD doses as soon as possible after dialysis.

HD model and sources

Replacement dose: Peak_req = Trough / e^(−K_r(gap − T_HD)), C_postHD = Trough·e^(−(K_r + K_HD)T_HD), Dose = (Peak_req − C_postHD)·Vd. Clearances are in L/h and not weight-scaled.

Weekly superposition: one dose at the end of each session, A = D / (Vd(1 − e^−(K_r·168 + K_HD·T_HD·3))); each peak sums the doses of the week decayed by renal K for the elapsed time plus HD K for each session passed. Trough before a session = previous peak × e^(−K_r(gap − T_HD)). The site prints the trough exponent as e^(−(Krenal*(3*24)−Tdialysis)); it is read here as K_r × (72 − T_HD), which reproduces the site's table.

Parameters: after HD Vd 1.03 L/kg, CL 0.4621 L/h, CL_HD 4.08 L/h (2020 MUE, institutional, unpublished); during HD 1.17 / 0.3348 / 6.9 (2016 MUE). Site model preset "Pierce HD workbook": Vd 1.0 L/kg (0.65/1/1.3), the same flat clearances with 0.25–2 multipliers, pre-HD 15, 56 h between sessions, 2 h infusion in the AUC (vancomycinHDClearnacnevrsweight.xlsx; clearances do not scale with weight). AUC regression AUC = 24.715 × trough + 53.958 is a site MUE estimate, not peer reviewed.

Literature (Rybak 2020, PMID 32191793): LD 25 mg/kg (35 intradialytic high-flux, 30 low-flux), cap 3 g (4 g option, Payne 2024, low evidence); MD 10 mg/kg TBW after HD (12.5 intradialytic; 7.5 low-flux), rounded to 250 mg with a 500 mg floor and 1500 mg cap (institutional, low confidence); 25% larger dose before the 3-day gap; pre-HD 15–20 (15–25 option for severe infection). First pre-HD level before the 2nd session after the load (Lewis 2021, UNMC 2024). Residual function: about 40 mg per mL/min per 48 h (Matzke-derived, ×1.5 for 72 h). Tool does not apply to CRRT or HD for acute kidney injury.

Pre-HD adjustment (Literature): Stanford Health Care Vancomycin Dosing Guide 2023; UNMC/Nebraska Medicine 2024; Panais R, J Nephrol 2010 (PMID 20540035); Lewis SJ, Mueller BA, J Clin Pharmacol 2021 (PMID 32851685) and Lewis 2023 BMC Nephrol (PMID 37710245). Proportional estimate = dose × goal midpoint ÷ level, bounded ±50%. Decisions: DECISIONS.md and decisions/vancomycin-aminoglycosides.md §1–5.
