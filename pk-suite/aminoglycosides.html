Methods, provenance and sign-off items
For education and clinical decision support by trained clinicians only. Outputs are one-compartment model estimates from population parameters and a few levels; they do not replace clinical judgement, local protocols or pharmacist review. Check level timing, renal trends, interacting drugs and audiology. Badges marked "verify" are values from outside the site or awaiting clinician sign-off.
