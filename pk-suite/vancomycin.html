Methods, sources and sign-off items

Targets. AUC24 400–600 mg·h/L assuming MIC 1 (Rybak 2020, PMID 32191793). AUC > 600 and troughs > 15–20 mg/L are associated with nephrotoxicity. Trough-only targeting of 15–20 is no longer recommended.

Literature model (default). 1-comp K = 0.00107·CrCl(mL/min/1.73 m²) + 0.0052 with Cockcroft-Gault on min(IBW, TBW) (the regression's own CrCl definition), Vd 0.65 L/kg TBW, or 0.5 L/kg TBW when BMI ≥ 30 with no TBW cap (Rybak 2020; Adane 2015), a warning above 200 kg, and an optional fixed 75 L at BMI ≥ 40 (Dunn 2019, PMID 31630155). Matzke 1984 (CL = 3.66 + 0.689·CrCl mL/min, Vd 0.7 L/kg) is an alternate. Loading dose 20–35 mg/kg TBW (≤ 25 if BMI ≥ 30), rounded to 250 mg, capped at 3000 mg (4000 mg option: Payne 2024, PMID 39493211, small RCT, low confidence). Maintenance = target AUC × CL × τ/24, rounded to 250 mg. Interval auto = ln 2/K (2-comp: K = CL/Vss) + T′ snapped to the nearest of 8/12/16/24/36/48/72 h. Empiric doses > 4500 mg/day are flagged.

Goti 2018 two-compartment (published, PMID 29470227; confirmed against Mena 2023, Clin Transl Sci 2023 and the InsightRX model code; primary paper not read, verify): CL = 4.5·(CrCl/120)^0.8·0.7^DIAL L/h, V1 = 58.4·(TBW/70)·0.5^DIAL L, V2 = 38.4 L, Q = 6.5 L/h; CrCl Cockcroft-Gault on TBW, capped at 150 mL/min. The original model sets SCr to 1.0 if age > 65; the 2019 erratum (PMID 31306395) changed the threshold from 60 to 65 according to an InsightRX code comment (erratum text not read, verify). Default off (modified Goti; Tong 2021, PMID 33009289, better in the elderly). IIV ω CL 0.398, V1 0.816, V2 0.571; residual 19.7% + 1.42 mg/L (InsightRX MAP-stable) by default, published 22.7% + 3.4 mg/L as an option. DIAL = 0 here (hemodialysis is in vancomycin-hd.html).

Site model. Non-Bayesian calculator as verified on the live server (2026-10-23): banker's rounding at every step with rounded values carried forward; SCr to 0.1, no floor; CrCl on min(IBW, TBW), Chow with creatinine Vd 0.6 × TBW when SCr changes; K = 0.00107·CrCl·1.73/BSA(0.01) + 0.00521 (fitted intercept; the documented 0.0052 mis-rounds two server rows), 4 dp; Vd 0.65 L/kg TBW; loading dose 40·Vd·K·T′/(1 − e^−KT′) with no 3 g cap; interval 0.7/K + T′ snapped up to 8/12/16/24/36/48/72/96/120; Cmin from the rounded Cmax; AUC = Dose/(K·Vd)·24/τ. The site Bayesian mode follows the site's web Bayesian page (not the Excel workbooks, see Workbook model): 0.0052 intercept unrounded, normal priors CV 0.3/0.3 on CL and V, assay CV 0.1 on the observed level. The site's "modified Goti" (Vc 58.4·WT/70, Vp 38.4·WT/70, Q 6.5, CV 0.3 on all parameters; unpublished) is offered in site mode only, in two versions: CL 6.4·(CrCl/120)^0.8 (current: 3/2023 MUE Part 2 PDF and the FractionSS workbook; default) and CL 6.04·(CrCl/120)^0.8 (legacy: 4/2022 MUE PDF and the site's web pages) verify. Literature mode keeps the published Goti CL 4.5. Carreno (site MUE 3/2023, site mode only): Vc 25.76 L, k12 2.29 and k21 1.44 h⁻¹ (Q 58.99 L/h, Vp 40.97 L), CL = 0.043292·CrCl + 0.331123 L/h (4/2022 version 0.038151·CrCl + 0.4339, recorded, not offered) with Cockcroft-Gault on TBW, or AdjBW when TBW ≥ 1.2·IBW. Its Bayesian priors are not documented: CV 0.3 on every parameter is assumed verify.

Workbook model (opt-in). Reproduces the site's Excel files as read from formula dumps (workbook-checks/vancomycin-aminoglycosides.md §1–2; the files were saved blank, so checks use re-implemented cases). Fitting workbook (K_VD_Fitting_V2; Initial and Bayesian tabs): SCr floored at 0.7 mg/dL (0.5 with paralysis or malnutrition) before the Chiou two-SCr fit (creatinine Vd 0.6·TBW, production on min(IBW, TBW), CrCl bounded 5–244 mL/min); K = 0.00107·CrCl·1.73/BSA + 0.005216005; Vd 0.65 L/kg TBW; loading dose for a 30 mg/L peak by the infusion formula (no cap; warning above 3 g); interval ln(30/15)/K + 2 h (fixed 2 h, not T′); maintenance 12.5 mg/kg TBW; AUC24 = [(Cmax + Cmin)·T′/2 + (Cmax − Cmin)/K]·24/τ. Fit: normal priors on K and V, SD 0.3 × population, residual SD 0.1 × observed; non-Bayesian = Σ(pred − obs)²; Solver box K 0.1–10 ×, V 0.5–2 × population; K-only fits hold V. AUC workbook (Levels tab, peak and trough): Cockcroft-Gault on min(IBW, TBW) with no floor; CL = (0.00107·CrCl/1.73 m² + 0.0052)·0.65·TBW whatever Vd (0.5/0.65/1 L/kg) is chosen; priors on CL and V with SD 0.3 × the fitted value; non-Bayesian = relative least squares; box 0.5–2 ×; AUC = Dose/CL·24/τ. Not copied (workbook errors): levels during an infusion are predicted here with the infusion equation, not the post-infusion one; the AUC workbook's Sawchuk-Zaske helper AUC ((Cmax + Cmin)·2/T′) is not used. Not built: per-dose SCr (time-varying K), the steady-state buttons that treat the measured trough as a known starting level, the 7-dose/14-level layout (larger histories are used and flagged), the pediatric branch.

Levels. Peak and trough after the same dose: K = ln(Cpk/Ctr)/Δt, Δt = τ − T′ − tpeak after end − ttrough before start; Vd by Sawchuk-Zaske; AUC24 = Dose/CL·24/τ and the linear-up/log-down trapezoid (Pai 2014). Trough only: K solved from one steady-state level with Vd fixed at the population value; less reliable (Rybak 2020; Neely 2014, trough-only data underestimated AUC by 23%).

Bayesian. MAP over the actual dose history by superposition (non-steady state allowed). Literature: log-normal priors ω 0.30 on CL and V (1-comp; approved, decisions §6) or the Goti IIV; combined residual σ = √((a·Ĉ)² + b²) on the predicted level (1-comp 15% + 1.5 mg/L; Goti 19.7% + 1.42 mg/L default), objective including ln σ². Site: Σ((obs − pred)/(CV·obs))² + Σ((θ − μ)/(0.3·μ))², normal priors. CL only is fitted for troughs or a single level; for 2-comp, Vc and CL are fitted (Vp also with ≥ 4 levels in Goti), Q held. The AUC range shown is ±1.96 approximate posterior SE of ln CL.

Local helpers (not in pkcore). Banker's rounding for site parity; bisection for K from one steady-state level; MAP with combined error on the predicted level (PK.fit weights only on the observed level). All other math uses PK.body, PK.renal, PK.one, PK.two, PK.fit and PK.round.

Decisions applied (DECISIONS.md, decisions/vancomycin-aminoglycosides.md, approved): obese Vd 0.5 L/kg TBW at BMI ≥ 30 with no TBW cap, warning > 200 kg, fixed 75 L option at BMI ≥ 40; LD cap 3 g (4 g option); MAP priors log-normal ω 0.30/0.30 with 15% + 1.5 mg/L on predicted; Goti confirmed, CG on TBW capped 150, elderly SCr rounding off. Still open: the Goti primary paper and erratum text have not been read.

Educational and clinical decision-support use by trained clinicians only. Population predictions are estimates; confirm with measured levels, renal trend and clinical judgement before dosing. Not a substitute for product labeling or institutional protocols. Data stay in this page. PK Suite .
