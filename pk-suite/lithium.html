Methods, model differences and sources
Educational and decision-support use by trained clinicians only. Not a substitute for clinical judgement. Population estimates of lithium clearance vary widely between patients; always confirm with a 12-hour level and monitor renal function, thyroid function, sodium and fluid status. Lithium toxicity can be life-threatening.
