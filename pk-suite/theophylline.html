Methods, model differences and sources

Structure (both models). One-compartment, linear. Dosing weight = lesser of Devine IBW and actual weight. V = 0.5 L/kg × dosing weight. CL = age-band CL (L/kg/h) × dosing weight × product of factor multipliers. K = CL/V, t½ = 0.693/K. F = 1 for every form. S divides doses (salt mg = theophylline mg ÷ S).

Dosing. Loading dose (Ctarget − Ccurrent)·V/S (partial load from a current level). Hourly dose / infusion rate Cavg·CL/S; daily = 24 × hourly. Predicted steady state by superposition of each dose of the 24-h schedule; trough is the level before the first dose of the day; average S·D·n/(CL·24).

K fit. V is fixed at the population value and K is solved exactly (bisection) so that the model reproduces the measured level: steady state (any schedule, or infusion) or non-steady state by superposition of the actual dose history (optional load, optional pre-existing level). The site's own solver is approximate (0.1–0.3% from the exact root).

Literature set (default), from lit/digoxin-theophylline.md: as approved in decisions/digoxin-theophylline.md (Q7–Q11): CL by age from the aminophylline label Table V, ≤ 9 y 0.08, ≤ 12 y 0.07, ≤ 16 y 0.05, ≤ 60 y 0.04 L/kg/h, > 60 y 0.028 (0.025 or 0.03 selectable); smoker ×1.4 (12–16 y), ×1.5 (16–60 y), ×1.8 (> 60 y); CHF ×0.5 (severe ×0.4); cirrhosis ×0.5; cystic fibrosis ×1.5; acute viral illness / fever ×0.6; pulmonary edema ×0.5; propranolol ×0.5, rifampin ×1.4, fluvoxamine ×0.33, phenobarbital ×1.33, phenytoin ×1.67, influenza vaccine ×1.0, label pick-list for others; aminophylline S 0.79 (every US injection, dihydrate) or 0.86 (bulk anhydrous powder only), oxtriphylline 0.64; sustained-release modelled as zero-order input over the dosing interval (Cpeak ≈ Ctrough ≈ Cavg); levels at the product's peak (IR / solution 1–2 h; 12-h and once-daily ER tablets 12 h after an evening or 9 h after a morning dose; Theo-24 12 h), never a trough; target Cavg 10 mg/L within 5–15; dose caps (≤ 400 mg/day theophylline and ≤ 17 mg/h IV if > 60 y, ≤ 400 mg/day with impaired clearance; children < 45 kg capped at 12–14 mg/kg/day, max 300 mg/day, then the label titration; increase ≤ 25% per step). Under 152 cm (60 in), actual weight is used because Devine IBW is not valid.

Site set, black-box verified against pkineticdrugdosing.com on 2026-10-23 (specs/theophylline.md): CL 0.08 (≤ 9 y), 0.07 (≤ 12 y), 0.05 (≤ 16 y), 0.04 L/kg/h, no elderly band; smoker replaces the base with max(band, 0.07); CHF ×0.4; cirrhosis ×0.5 (the site label wrongly says "increase"); influenza vaccine ×0.5; propranolol ×0.6, rifampin ×1.3, phenobarbital ×1.3, phenytoin ×1.6; aminophylline S 0.84; sustained-release first-order absorption ka 0.18 h⁻¹; IR, elixir and injection as IV bolus; display rounding as on the site.

Not modelled. Neonates and infants < 1 year, pregnancy, Bayesian fitting, saturable elimination at high levels, renal function (theophylline is hepatically cleared; the site's SCr inputs are unused).
