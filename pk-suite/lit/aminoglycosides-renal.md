# Literature cross-check: aminoglycosides and shared renal/body-size equations

Scope: `specs/ag-traditional.md`, `specs/ag-once-daily.md`, `specs/crcl.md`, `specs/core-equations.md` §5, README §2–5. Checked 2026-10-23.

Method. Primary papers were located and their metadata or abstracts read through PubMed (E-utilities, via the PubMed connector). Guidance came from CLSI and FDA web pages. Textbook coefficients come from a secondary summary (GlobalRPh), because the textbooks themselves were not accessible. The specs were not edited.

Evidence tags:
- **[V]** I read the abstract or full text and it supports the claim.
- **[V-meta]** The paper exists (PMID and DOI confirmed), but the specific number is not in the abstract. Reading the full text is still needed.
- **[2nd]** Verified only through a secondary source.
- **[R]** Recalled, not verified in this session. Do not cite it as verified.

Full-text PDFs for Nicolau 1995 (the PMC scan has no text layer) and Urban & Craig 1997 (not in PubMed) could not be read. Hartford, Urban-Craig and Barnes-Jewish nomogram details are therefore partly [R].

## 3. Items that need clinician sign-off

1. **EID default dose 7 vs 5 mg/kg** for gent/tobra. The site uses 5, while the CLSI 2023 breakpoints assume 7 (E1).
2. **Obesity threshold** (1.2 vs 1.3·IBW vs BMI ≥ 30) for using AdjBW in CrCl. Also whether to use Janmahasatian LBW in CG at BMI ≥ 40, given that Winter 2012 and Demirovic 2009 conflict (R4, R5).
3. **SCr floor policy.** Default off, or 0.7 / 0.8 for AG K prediction (R9). The evidence is directly conflicting.
4. **Trough targets.** EID gent/tobra < 0.3 (site) vs < 0.5–1. Amikacin EID < 0.9 / 1 / 2.5. Traditional gent trough < 1 vs < 1.5–2 (A7, E6).
5. **Interval brackets.** Lower EID limit 30 (site) vs 20 (Hartford) (E2).
6. **Which nomogram ships.** Hartford needs its line coordinates digitised from Nicolau 1995 Fig. 1. That was **not done**; the full text was not machine-readable. Keep or drop the author's 55/35/25 curves (E3).
7. **Critically ill Vd override** value, if one is offered (A2).
8. **AUC maxima** (117/127/165/172/353/381): keep them as advisory or drop them (A9).
9. **IDSA 2026 aminoglycoside statements** to quote in help text (E11). Not read in this session.
